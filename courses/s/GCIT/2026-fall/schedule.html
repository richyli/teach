2026 GCIT — AI 創意與深度使用方法論　2026-fall

W1　9/14　Using AI Deeply

  • M1 Mindsets for Deep AI Use (AI as a thinking partner; prompting & dialogue design; reading AI output critically)
  • M2 Learning with AI (intro to 11 Learn-with-AI methods: Feynman, Socratic, active recall, protégé effect…)
  • M3 Method Try-out & Feedback (each student runs different learning methods on a topic, peer feedback)
  • 📝 Run one Learn-with-AI method on a topic of your choice; submit a short reflection on which methods you tried and which you'll keep

W2　9/21　Image Creativity

  • M1 Boden's Three Types × AI Image (combinational / explorative / transformational on one theme)
  • M2 Systematic Exploration of Concept Space (explorative; map the conceptual space)
  • M3 Prompting Methodology & Reverse-Inferring Assumptions (the thinking behind prompts; read a generated image to surface its hidden assumptions)
  • 📝 Submit three-type image comparison + three hidden assumptions reverse-inferred from one AI image

W3　10/5　Methodology: Reframing

  • M1 Using AI to List & Challenge Assumptions (AI lists assumptions; you judge which to break)
  • M2 Problem-finding & Jobs-to-be-Done (problem-finding is harder than problem-solving)
  • M3 Seeing Your Own Hidden Assumptions (full Harrison reframing on your own familiar domain)
  • 📝 Produce a one-page "Problem Redefinition Brief"

W4　10/12　Solving Social Problems & Ethics

  • M1 Systems Thinking for Social Problems (wicked problems; intervention levels & leverage points)
  • M2 Building a Causal Feedback Loop with AI (map a Taiwan social issue + 3 intervention points)
  • M3 Ethics & Boundaries of Creativity (AI copyright, style mimicry, training-data politics; draw your line)
  • 📝 A causal-loop diagram + 3 intervention points for a Taiwan social problem, plus one sentence on your AI-creativity ethical boundary

W5　10/19　Midterm Presentation

  • M1 Proposal Design Workshop (pick one track: expression / business / social)
  • M2 Peer Review & Feedback (rubric-based)
  • M3 Method Statement & Reflection — present + method statement + Q&A
  • 📝 Final work: artifact/proposal + method statement ("which hidden assumption did you break, and what role did AI play?")
