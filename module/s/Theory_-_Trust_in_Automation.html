Trust in Automation

A short primer on Trust in Automation: what it claims, where it sits in this course, and the questions it lets you ask about your own product.

John D. Lee & Katrina A. See (2004) — "Calibrate trust to capability — miscalibrated trust kills."

The core idea

Trust is "the attitude that an agent will help achieve an individual's goals in a situation characterized by uncertainty and vulnerability."

Three attributes users read to form trust: - Purpose — why was this system built? (designer intent) - Process — how does it work? (mechanism, fairness) - Performance — does it work? (accuracy, reliability)

Three cognitive routes: - Analytic (slow, evidence-based) ↔ Analogical (rule-of-thumb, category match) ↔ Affective (gut feeling)

The goal is appropriate reliance — neither over-trust (misuse) nor under-trust (disuse).

Lens questions

When you face an AI feature decision, ask: 1. Which of purpose / process / performance does the user actually have evidence for? 2. Does the UI surface uncertainty, or hide it to look "smart"? 3. Are users currently in misuse (over-trust), disuse (under-trust), or appropriate reliance? 4. Is trust forming through analytic evidence, or only affect and analogy? 5. What signal would recalibrate trust after the model changes?

Pair with Agentic AI

Three concrete moves for AI-assisted PM work: 1. Trust-calibration UI — surface AI confidence + provenance + scope of competence on every output, not buried in settings 2. Vibe-Coding as calibration drill — log every accept/reject decision and the reason; the log is your personal calibration curve 3. Re-baseline on model upgrade — when the model changes, old trust is now miscalibrated trust; force a recalibration session

⚠️ Pitfall: hiding AI uncertainty to make the product feel "smart" trains users into miscalibrated trust — the next high-stakes failure is then yours, not the model's.

Discussion

  1. Pick one AI feature you ship today. Are users in misuse, disuse, or appropriate reliance — and what evidence do you have?
  2. If you had to surface one trust signal in the UI tomorrow — purpose, process, or performance — which moves the needle most for your product, and why?
