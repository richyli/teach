Wireframe and the Fidelity Ladder

This unit covers the user-story format, the low-to-high fidelity ladder, and what gets quietly lost each time a design is handed to someone else.

Core

  • User-story format: As a… I want… so that…
  • Wireframe tools (Figma, Excalidraw); the fidelity ladder from low to mid to high.
  • AI-generated wireframes and how they fail.
  • Primary theory: Affordance Theory — designing a wireframe is designing perceived affordances. Supporting: Sociomateriality, Boundary Object Theory.

Hands-on — Wireframe Telephone

  1. Team A draws a low-fidelity wireframe
  2. Team B reinterprets it at mid fidelity
  3. Team C builds it at high fidelity
  4. It returns to Team A, and everyone marks which affordances survived each handoff and which got distorted

✍️ Take-home task

Submit the three fidelity levels side by side, plus an analysis of what got distorted.

💬 Discussion and reflection

Pair review

Look at each other's stage of the wireframe.

  1. Say what you read from the drawing — what does it want the user to do?
  2. Compare that with what the author intended
  3. Mark which affordances were transmitted accurately and which were misread

The point is not whether the drawing is attractive. It is whether it lets someone work out how to operate the thing.

Individual reflection

Write one sentence to keep: "If I generated a wireframe with AI, the stage I would least want to hand over is __, because ____."
