Glossary｜術語表

One card per term, English above and 中文 below. Front-stage content links here the first time a term appears.

一個詞一頁，上面英文、下面中文。前台內容第一次出現這些詞時會連過來。

Term 中文 In one line｜一句話
Acceptance Criteria 驗收條件 The specific conditions a requirement has to satisfy before it counts as done — written by the product side, checked by engineering.
一條需求要滿足哪些具體條件，才算做完——由產品端寫，工程端照著驗。
BRD 商業需求文件 The document that argues why the company should spend money on this at all — written for executives, usually before any product exists.
說明「公司為什麼該把錢花在這件事上」的文件，寫給高階主管看，通常在產品還不存在時就寫。
Functional Spec 功能規格書 The engineering-side description of how a requirement will actually be implemented.
從工程角度描述「這條需求實際上要怎麼被實作」的文件。
MRD 市場需求文件 The document that argues a market exists and is worth entering — written for the people who decide whether to fund it.
說明「這個市場存在、而且值得投入」的文件，寫給決定要不要出錢的人看。
PRD 產品需求文件 The document that says what gets built and how you will know it is done — written for the people who build it.
說明「要做什麼、做到什麼程度算完成」的文件，寫給要動手做的人看。
Persona 人物誌 A fictional person, written from real research, specific enough that a team can use them to settle an argument.
把一群真實使用者的共同樣貌，寫成一個具體到可以拿來做決定的虛構人物。
Product Lifecycle 產品生命週期 The full loop a product runs through — research, documents, build, launch, metrics — where every stage owes the next one something concrete.
一個產品從研究、寫文件、做出來、上線到看數字的完整循環，每一階都要交一樣東西給下一階。
User Journey 使用者旅程 The path a person takes from not knowing your product exists to deciding whether to come back, broken into stages you can observe separately.
把一個人從不知道你的產品、到用完之後會不會回來，拆成一段一段可以分別觀察的過程。
User Story 使用者故事 One requirement written as "as a ⟨kind of user⟩ I want ⟨to do something⟩ so that ⟨I get some result⟩".
把一條需求寫成「身為〈某種使用者〉，我想要〈做某件事〉，以便〈得到某個結果〉」的一句話。
Wireframe 線框圖 A deliberately ugly layout sketch that shows structure and behaviour, not colour and type.
刻意做得很醜的版面草圖，只表達結構與行為，不表達顏色與字體。
