Audience Research and the Mom Test

This unit covers personas and segmentation, the ethics of AI-driven personalisation, and how to interview someone so that you get their behaviour instead of their compliments.

Core

  • Persona, segmentation, psychographic versus demographic.
  • The ethics of AI-driven personalisation; algorithmic construction of audiences.
  • Primary theory: Uses and Gratifications. Supporting: Para-social Interaction, Algorithmic Imaginaries (Bucher) — what users think the algorithm is doing matters as much as what it actually does.

Hands-on — Mom Test Live

Work in pairs. Follow Rob Fitzpatrick's rules in The Mom Test: do not ask for opinions, do not state your assumptions, do not pitch.

  1. Interview each other about the last time you consumed a piece of content
  2. Mark which questions surfaced real behaviour and which only produced sympathy
  3. Keep the wording of both kinds, because you are handing them in

This sets up the comparison for the AI-persona focus group in W13.

✍️ Take-home task

Submit three questions that surfaced real behaviour and three that did not, side by side.

💬 Discussion and reflection

Pair review

Swap your interview questions with the person next to you.

  1. Find the question that was really leading them, or fishing for reassurance
  2. Rewrite it for them as a question that asks only about a specific thing they did in the past
  3. Say what the rewritten version would have caught that the original missed

Individual reflection

Write one sentence to keep: "Next time I interview my target audience, the habitual bad question I will drop is __, and I will replace it with ____."
