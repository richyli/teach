PRD Writing and Stress Test

This unit covers PRD structure, acceptance criteria and edge cases, and the new risk that an AI agent fills your under-specified spec with a plausible but wrong default instead of asking you a question.

Core

  • PRD structure, acceptance criteria, edge cases.
  • A development philosophy for the age of Agentic AI.
  • Primary theory: Boundary Object Theory — the PRD encodes shared rules across the activity system and freezes one sensemaking outcome. Supporting: Activity Theory (rules), Sensemaking. Together with the W7 MRD, this gives you two contrasting boundary objects in one semester.

A real PRD to work from: Tekton / Yingzao

The full text is in Tekton PRD (Build Day Winner), from Claude Build Day Hackathon. A real, award-winning PRD of about 6,500 words in 13 sections — far more useful as a model than an abstract list of headings.

The 13 sections: One-Line Punch · Product Summary · Why This Matters · Founder Story · Subject Buildings · UX (six layers, explicitly Not a Chatbot) · Reconstruction Pipeline (five agent sub-sections) · Build-Day Orchestration · Data Sources & Rights · MVP Scope · Tech Stack · Definition of Done (nine items) · Submission Checklist.

The One-Line Punch is a reframing. "Make right cheaper than looks-like" — the first sentence of the PRD is a redefinition of the problem.

An explicit not-doing list. Section 10 forbids a chat box, accounts, a CMS, and more than three buildings. That is a model piece of anti-scope-creep, and it leads into Scope Management and Prioritisation.

Acceptance is settled in advance, not chosen on the day. Colours are frozen as design tokens beforehand and deployment produces frozen verified artifacts with no runtime LLM — which is what Section 12's Definition of Done is enforcing.

Note what is not in it. The 50 work tickets live on a Notion board, not in the PRD. The spec and the tickets are two different boundary objects.

Hands-on — PRD Stress Test

  1. Team A writes a PRD
  2. Team B hands it to an AI agent and ships whatever the agent produces
  3. Team C reviews the output and finds every spec gap and tacit assumption

✍️ Take-home task

Submit the repaired PRD plus the list of gaps found.

💬 Discussion and reflection

Pair review

Swap PRDs with a partner and play a malicious AI agent.

  1. Find sentences that would produce the wrong thing if followed literally
  2. Find the edge cases that were never written down
  3. Find the tacit assumption hiding underneath
  4. Mark at least two gaps each

Individual reflection

Write one sentence to keep: "The place my PRD is most likely to be misread by an AI is __, and next time I will pin it down with ____" — acceptance criteria, a not-doing list, or a pre-frozen token.
