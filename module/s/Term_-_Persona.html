Persona｜人物誌

Persona

A fictional person, written from real research, specific enough that a team can use them to settle an argument.

把一群真實使用者的共同樣貌，寫成一個具體到可以拿來做決定的虛構人物。

What this term means｜這個詞在說什麼

The point of a persona is to end arguments: when the team is fighting about a feature, a persona lets you ask "would this person use it?" instead of "what do you think?".

A useful persona comes out of interviews and data, and carries goals, context, constraints, and what the person does today instead. A persona with no research behind it is the team's own assumptions in disguise — which is worse than having none, because it makes a guess look like evidence.

Persona 的用途是在爭論時終止爭論：當團隊為了要不要做某個功能吵起來，persona 讓你問「這個人會用嗎」，而不是「我覺得」。

有用的 persona 來自訪談與資料，包含目標、情境、限制、既有替代方案。沒有訪談基礎的 persona 是團隊自己的投影，那比沒有 persona 更危險，因為它會讓錯誤的假設看起來像證據。

Common misconceptions｜常見誤解

  • Mistaking demographics for a persona. Age and job title do not tell you why this person would use your product.

  • Building five personas at once. Most student projects need one primary persona and one clearly excluded group.

  • 把人口統計當成 persona。年齡與職業不會告訴你他為什麼要用你的產品。

  • 一次做五個 persona。多數學生專案只需要一個主要 persona 和一個明確排除的對象。
