Technology Acceptance Model

A short primer on Technology Acceptance Model / UTAUT: what it claims, where it sits in this course, and the questions it lets you ask about your own product.

Davis (1989); Venkatesh, Morris, Davis & Davis (2003); Venkatesh, Thong & Xu (2012) — "Perceived usefulness × perceived ease of use → intention → use."

The core idea

Adoption is predicted by two beliefs: Perceived Usefulness (PU) and Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU) → Behavioural Intention → Actual Use.

Evolutions: - TAM (1989) — PU + PEOU → BI → Use - UTAUT (2003) — 4 predictors (performance expectancy, effort expectancy, social influence, facilitating conditions) × 4 moderators (gender, age, experience, voluntariness) - UTAUT2 (2012) — adds hedonic motivation, price value, habit

The most-cited adoption framework in IS research.

Lens questions

When you face an adoption problem, ask: 1. Is the bottleneck PU (people don't see the point) or PEOU (people can't get through the door)? 2. Which moderator dominates here — voluntariness? experience? 3. Is hedonic motivation (UTAUT2) doing the actual work in our product? 4. Have we confused intention with use?

Recommended reading

  • Davis 1989 Perceived Usefulness, MIS Quarterly 13(3) — the original TAM
  • Venkatesh 2003 User Acceptance, MIS Quarterly 27(3) — UTAUT, unified model
  • Venkatesh, Thong & Xu 2012, MIS Quarterly 36(1) — UTAUT2, consumer context

(See also: Venkatesh 2022 Adoption Use AI for AI-era extension)

Pair with Agentic AI

Three concrete moves for AI-assisted PM work: 1. Instant UTAUT survey — have an AI agent draft a UTAUT-style instrument from your PRD in minutes, for early-stage feature validation 2. Belief-mapping interviews — use AI to code open-ended interview transcripts into PU / PEOU / hedonic / habit buckets 3. Friction triage — feed support tickets to AI, ask it to classify each as a PU vs PEOU complaint

⚠️ Failure mode: TAM does not capture trust in AI, which often dominates AI adoption. Pair with Trust-in-Automation theory or risk under-modelling.

Discussion

  1. For an AI agent that books your meetings, would PU or trust be the binding constraint? Can UTAUT absorb trust as just another belief?
  2. UTAUT2 added habit. For AI tools that change weekly, is "habit" still a meaningful variable?
