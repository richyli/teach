Risk Management and Pre-mortem

This unit covers the risk register, the risks that are specific to AI products, the regulatory picture in Taiwan and the EU, and a pre-mortem you run with an agent as a second pair of eyes.

Core

  • The risk register.
  • AI-specific risks: hallucination, model drift, prompt injection, data leakage.
  • Regulatory risk: the EU AI Act, and Taiwan's AI Basic Act (in force since 14 January 2026).
  • Reputational risk.
  • Primary theory: Sensemaking (Weick on high-reliability organisations). Supporting: Effectuation (affordable loss), Trust in Automation — HRO thinking, affordable loss, and miscalibrated trust together cover risk in the AI era.

Hands-on — Pre-mortem with AI

  1. Assume the product is dead six months from now, and list ten causes of death
  2. Have an Agentic AI add five more that you missed
  3. Rank them by severity × likelihood and design a mitigation for the top three

✍️ Take-home task

Submit the ranked risk table plus mitigations for the top three risks.

💬 Discussion and reflection

Pair review

Swap ranked risk tables with a partner.

  1. Find one cause of death whose severity × likelihood score you think is wrong, and say how you would change it
  2. Check whether they listed the AI-specific risks — hallucination, drift, prompt injection, data leakage
  3. Name the risk they are most likely to be underrating because it is uncomfortable

Individual reflection

Write one sentence to keep: "After the pre-mortem, the risk that worries me most about my own product is __, and the step I will take before next week to reduce it is ____."
