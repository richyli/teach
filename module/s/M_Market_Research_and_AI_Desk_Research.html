Market Research and AI Desk Research

This unit covers three frameworks for reading a market, how to run desk research with an AI agent, and how to read a research report critically enough that it cannot sell you something.

Core

  • PEST, Five Forces, VRIO.
  • AI-assisted desk research with Perplexity, Deep Research, and Gemini Deep Research.
  • How to read a research report critically.
  • Primary theory: Sensemaking (Weick) — market research is collective meaning-making under equivocality. Supporting: Dynamic Capabilities (sensing), Technology Acceptance Model.

Reading a research report critically means checking three things

  1. Who was sampled — how large the population is, and how the sample was drawn.
  2. What the numbers mean — the definition and the time window. Is "30% growth" year on year or month on month, and how low was the base?
  3. Who paid for it — consultancies publish reports that clear a path for their own services.

Where AI desk research goes wrong

Perplexity and Deep Research will hand you stale figures, marketing copy, and occasionally a hallucinated citation, all delivered with total confidence. Open every source link and verify it line by line, and require the agent to mark which statements are inference with no source behind them.

Hands-on — Equivocal Market Brief

Your team receives a market dossier that is deliberately vague and partly self-contradictory.

  1. Use an Agentic AI to build a market narrative out of it
  2. Mark every claim as either sourced or inferred
  3. Next week the ground truth is revealed, and you find out whose narrative held up

✍️ Take-home task

Submit your market narrative with each claim marked as AI inference or sourced.

💬 Discussion and reflection

Pair review

Swap Equivocal Market Brief outputs with another team.

  1. Find the least defensible conclusion in their narrative
  2. Ask whether it is AI inference or sourced
  3. If it is sourced, ask whether the source is credible, and say why

Individual reflection

Write one sentence to keep: "Next time I use AI for desk research I will add one step to stop myself being convinced by a fake number, namely ______" — going back to original links every time, or listing the conclusion you most want to be true and then hunting for counter-evidence.
