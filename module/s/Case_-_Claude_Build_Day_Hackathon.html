Case Study — What Designing Products with AI Actually Takes (Claude Opus 4.8 Build Day Hackathon)

案例：用 AI 設計產品真正需要什麼能力

For 2026 Product Planning Syllabus — suggested as a Week 1 opener ("why this course") or supplementary reading. 供 2026 Product Planning Syllabus 使用——建議當 W1 開場「為什麼學這門課」案例，或某週補充讀本。 Source 來源：Meet the winners of our Claude Opus 4.8 Build Day hackathon（2026-06-13, San Francisco）

The event 活動

EN — On 13 June 2026, 310 builders (out of 1,500 applicants) spent 12 hours in San Francisco each shipping a product, with US$500 of credits per team. Three winners stood out:

  • 🥇 Tekton (Holly Tang & Austin Burgess) — 3D-reconstructs historical buildings (Tang-dynasty architecture to the Notre-Dame spire), tracing every component back to a historical source. Opus 4.8 verified the whole reconstruction; independent verification sub-agents scored in isolated contexts; a self-correction loop checked component placement until 20 tests passed, across 339 incremental build stages.
  • 🥈 Sim Francisco (Tanmayi Priya Dasari & Tejas Prabhune) — a digital twin of San Francisco: 10,000 synthetic residents based on US Census data that react to news in real time and predict polls. Predicted the 2024 presidential result at 81.3% vs an actual 83.8%, and SF Prop A at 70% vs 70.38%. An evolutionary clustering algorithm cut 10,000 residents to 300 representatives, dropping inference cost 10–100×.
  • 🥉 Custom Universe (Jake Stevens & Mauricio Pereira) — turns a phone photo into a real-time, editable, photorealistic 3D scene; targets robotics labs needing synthetic training data. Built end-to-end with Opus 4.8; ran models on a remote NVIDIA H100; integrated Apple RealityKit; open-sourced the models so users can run them on their own GPUs.

Judges rewarded: verifiability, real-world predictive impact, and practical deployability.

中 — 2026 年 6 月 13 日，310 位建構者（1,500 人申請）在舊金山用 12 小時各做出一個產品，每隊 500 美元額度。三個得獎作品：

  • 🥇 Tekton——3D 重建歷史建築（唐代建築到聖母院尖塔），把每個組件追溯到史料來源。Opus 4.8 驗證整個重建過程，獨立驗證子代理在隔離上下文評分，自修正迴圈反覆檢查到通過 20 項測試，共 339 個增量構建階段。
  • 🥈 Sim Francisco——舊金山數位孿生：一萬名基於人口普查的合成居民，實時對新聞回應並預測民調。2024 總統大選預測 81.3%、實際 83.8%；SF Prop A 預測 70%、實際 70.38%。演化式聚類把一萬人縮到 300 代表，推理成本降低 10–100 倍。
  • 🥉 Custom Universe——手機照片轉實時可編輯的光真實感 3D 場景，瞄準機器人實驗室的合成訓練資料需求。用 Opus 4.8 端對端建構，遠端操作 NVIDIA H100，整合 Apple RealityKit，開源模型讓使用者在自己的 GPU 跑。

評審看重：可驗證性、真實影響預測力、實用可部署性。

The point 重點

EN — None of the three won because AI wrote more code for them. AI writing code is the baseline, not the differentiator. Every edge came from human judgement — exactly the abilities this course teaches, none of which are "coding."

中 — 三隊沒有一個是贏在「AI 幫他們寫了多少 code」。AI 寫 code 是前提，不是差異。每個勝出點都來自人帶進來的判斷——正是這門課在教的能力，沒有一項是「寫程式」。

Five abilities, mapped to the syllabus 五個能力，對應課綱

1. Plan before you build — PRD as a boundary object｜先規劃再建造——PRD 即邊界物件

→ W7 (MRD), W11 (PRD)

EN — Tekton's first advice: "Map the whole project before you build any of it." They wrote a 50-ticket PRD and a Notion board before letting AI start. In the Agentic-AI era, the spec is not bureaucracy — it is the boundary object between you and the AI. AI fills whatever you leave under-specified with plausible-but-wrong defaults (the whole point of the W11 PRD Stress Test). Tekton controlled the AI with the spec, instead of being dragged along by it.

中 — Tekton 的第一條建議：「動工前先把整個專案規劃完。」他們先寫好 50 張工單的 PRD 和 Notion 看板才讓 AI 動手。在 Agentic AI 時代，規格不是官僚文書，而是你和 AI 之間的邊界物件。AI 會用「看似合理但錯誤」的預設填補你沒寫清楚的地方（這就是 W11 PRD Stress Test 的重點）。Tekton 是用規格控制 AI，不是被 AI 牽著走。

2. Don't settle for the first working solution — scope & effectuation｜不滿足於第一個可行解——範疇與 effectuation

→ W12 (Scope Management)

EN — Sim Francisco's advice: don't settle for the first method that works, especially when it's expensive. Running inference on all 10,000 residents worked but cost too much, so they clustered down to 300 representatives — a 10–100× cost cut. When AI makes "cost of building" low, the scarce resource becomes judgement: knowing which working solution shouldn't survive. AI won't tell you "this is too expensive" — that's the PM's job.

中 — Sim Francisco 的建議：不要滿足於第一個有效的方法，尤其當它很貴。對一萬人直接跑推理能動但太貴，於是他們聚類縮到 300 代表，成本降 10–100 倍。當 AI 讓「建造成本」變低，真正稀缺的就是判斷力——知道哪個能跑的解不該留。AI 不會主動說「這太貴」，那是 PM 的工作。

3. Calibrate trust — verification & adversarial agents｜校準信任——驗證與對抗代理

→ W10 (Trust in Automation), W13 (Synthetic-data validity)

EN — Both winners designed mechanisms to distrust the AI's output. Tekton used independent verification sub-agents and a self-correction loop until tests passed — an evidence chain. Sim Francisco had Claude work with "verification and adversarial agents" to keep demographics accurate, and back-tested against real election results. Sim Francisco's 10,000-resident poll is basically a top-scoring version of the W13 "Synthetic Focus Group vs Real Interview" exercise: synthetic data is trusted only after real-world back-testing, not because the AI says so.

中 — 兩個得獎隊都設計了「不信任 AI 輸出」的機制。Tekton 用獨立驗證子代理和自修正迴圈反覆檢查到通過測試——做成證據鏈。Sim Francisco 讓 Claude 跟「驗證代理和對抗代理」協作確保人口統計準確，並用真實選舉結果回測。Sim Francisco 的一萬人民調幾乎是 W13「合成焦點團體 vs 真人訪談」練習的滿分版：合成資料要拿真實世界回測才能信，不是 AI 說準就準。

4. Use AI to choose tools, not just write code — AI as co-planner｜用 AI 選工具，不只寫程式——AI 當共同規劃者

→ W2 / W10 (AI skill craftsmanship)

EN — Custom Universe put it bluntly: "Use Claude to choose tools, not just to write code. For example, Apple RealityKit, and how we'd let people input scanned objects — we asked Claude: add this to the pipeline." AI is not a typewriter, it's a working partner: you make architecture and tech-stack decisions with it. People who can design products with AI ask "is this the right path?", not "write me this function."

中 — Custom Universe 講得最直白：「用 Claude 選工具，不只寫程式。例如 Apple RealityKit、要怎麼讓使用者上傳掃描物件——我們問 Claude：把這個加進 pipeline。」AI 不是打字機，是工作夥伴：你跟它一起做架構與技術棧決策。會用 AI 設計產品的人問的是「這條路徑對嗎」，不是「幫我寫這個函式」。

5. The judges' criteria = your final Judge Skill｜評審標準＝你期末的 Judge Skill

→ W8 / W16 (Pitch + Judge Skill)

EN — The judges scored on verifiability, real predictive impact, and deployability — almost one-to-one with the course's Final Pitch + Judge Skill dimensions (viability / theoretical grounding / AI craftsmanship / scope discipline). The course 評分標準 isn't academic self-indulgence; industry judges "good AI-era products" by the same set. Designing a Judge Skill to evaluate other teams is the skill of stating clearly, in executable form, what "good" means.

中 — 評審看的是可驗證性、真實影響預測力、可部署性——幾乎一一對應課程期末 Final Pitch + Judge Skill 的維度（可行性 / 理論基礎 / AI 工藝 / 範疇紀律）。課程 評分標準 不是學院自嗨，業界用同一組標準評斷「AI 時代的好產品」。設計 Judge Skill 評他組，練的就是把「什麼叫好產品」明確寫成可執行的判準。

One-line takeaway 一句話總結

EN — When AI makes "buildable" cheap, the centre of gravity of product skill shifts entirely to what AI can't do: plan (PRD), make trade-offs (scope), distrust wisely (verification), treat AI as a co-planner (tool decisions), and articulate what "good" means (評分標準). AI finished 12 hours of coding for you; these five things, no one does for you. That is what 16 weeks of this course trains.

中 — 當 AI 把「做得出來」變便宜，產品能力的重心就整個移到 AI 做不到的地方：先規劃（PRD）、會取捨（範疇）、不輕信（驗證）、把 AI 當共同規劃者（工具決策）、講清楚什麼叫好（評分標準）。 寫 code 12 小時 AI 幫你做完了；這五件事沒有人幫你。這就是這門課十六週在練的東西。
