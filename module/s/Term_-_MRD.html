MRD｜市場需求文件

Market Requirement Document

The document that argues a market exists and is worth entering — written for the people who decide whether to fund it.

說明「這個市場存在、而且值得投入」的文件，寫給決定要不要出錢的人看。

What this term means｜這個詞在說什麼

An MRD answers should we do this at all, not how do we build it. The people it has to convince hold the budget; they do not write code.

A defensible MRD carries: market size with sources, a competitive comparison, the target segment and its persona, the user journey, and a positioning statement. Without the sizing and the sources, everything after it is just opinion.

MRD 回答的是該不該做，不是怎麼做。它要說服的人握著預算，不寫程式。

一份站得住的 MRD 至少要有：市場規模與來源、競品比較、目標客群與 persona、使用者旅程、定位與價值主張。缺了規模與來源那一段，後面都只是意見。

Common misconceptions｜常見誤解

  • Writing the MRD as a feature list. Features belong in the PRD; they appear in an MRD only as evidence that somebody needs them.

  • Using "everyone thinks" as market evidence. A number with no source counts as no number in this course.

  • 把 MRD 寫成功能清單。功能是 PRD 的事，MRD 出現功能只為了說明「有人需要」。

  • 用「大家都覺得」當市場證據。沒有來源的數字在這門課裡等於沒有數字。
