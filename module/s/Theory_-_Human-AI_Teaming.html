Human-AI Teaming

A short primer on Human-AI Teaming / Centaur Model: what it claims, where it sits in this course, and the questions it lets you ask about your own product.

Garry Kasparov (2010, 2017); Daugherty & Wilson (2018) — "Best human + best AI > best human or best AI alone."

The core idea

Complementary cognition — divide the task by who does each sub-task best, not by who is "smarter overall."

  • Origin: centaur chess (Kasparov, post-Deep Blue) — a mid-tier human paired with a mid-tier engine beats a grandmaster and beats a top engine alone
  • Modern operating modes:
  • Human-in-the-loop — human approves each step
  • Human-on-the-loop — human supervises, intervenes on exception
  • Human-out-of-the-loop — fully autonomous, human only audits
  • Task allocation is a design choice, not a default

Lens questions

When you design any AI-augmented workflow, ask: 1. What is each sub-task, and who is currently best at it — human, AI, or together? 2. Where am I assuming "the AI does X" without writing it down? 3. Which loop pattern fits this risk level — in, on, or out? 4. When the model upgrades next quarter, which of these allocations becomes obsolete? 5. What does the human uniquely contribute — context, accountability, judgement, taste?

Pair with Agentic AI

Three concrete moves for AI-assisted PM work: 1. Write the centaur down — explicitly map who does what between you and Claude in every workflow; an unwritten allocation is an unmanaged one 2. Bi-weekly rebalance — schedule a 30-min review every 2 weeks; AI capability shifts faster than you think 3. Pick the loop intentionally — for each sub-task choose in-the-loop (high stakes), on-the-loop (medium), or out-of-the-loop (low) — and name the audit hook

⚠️ Pitfall: treating the centaur as stable. After a major model upgrade, your previous task allocation is probably obsolete — keep using it and you ship a worse product than the rebalanced version.

Discussion

  1. In your current workflow with Claude, name three sub-tasks you do that the AI now does better — and why haven't you reallocated?
  2. Which loop pattern (in / on / out) does your product expose to end users, and is that the right one for the risk level?
