The pattern 共同模式

EN — Across all six, the technical wins (multi-agent debugging, vision reasoning, Managed Agents) are the means. The repeated lesson in every team's advice is the interaction method — how a human directs, distrusts, and collaborates with the AI. That is exactly what this course teaches, and it is more transferable than any single tool.

中 — 六隊的技術亮點（多代理除錯、視覺推理、Managed Agents）都只是手段。每隊建議裡反覆出現的，是互動工法——人怎麼指揮、不輕信、並與 AI 協作。這正是這門課要教的，也比任何單一工具更能遷移。

Six interaction principles, mapped to the syllabus 六條互動原則，對應課綱

1. Treat Claude as a thinking partner, not just a coding agent｜把 Claude 當思考夥伴，不只是編碼代理

Medkit → W2 / W10

EN — Medkit's founder first wanted to self-host the voice engine; Claude advised a cloud service to move faster, and he took it. The AI shaped an architecture decision, not just code. 中 — Medkit 作者本想自架語音引擎，Claude 建議改用雲端服務以加快速度，他採納了。AI 影響的是架構決策，不只是寫程式。

2. Think hard while brainstorming — and dare to push back｜腦力激盪要深想，並敢推回 AI 的建議

Wrench Board → W10 (calibrated trust) / W12 (negotiation)

EN — Wrench Board used a structured brainstorming framework, sometimes running several brainstorms in parallel, and insisted on pushing back when the model said "no." Calibrated trust runs both ways: lean on AI, but override it. 中 — Wrench Board 用結構化腦力激盪框架，有時並行多場，並在模型說「不行」時堅持推回。校準信任是雙向的：倚賴 AI，但該覆蓋時要覆蓋。

3. Think before you build｜先想再建

Maieutic → W7 (MRD) / W11 (PRD)

EN — Maieutic's founder spent two full days on design and technical spec with zero code — and that "slow" start made everything after faster. (Note: Maieutic's whole product is this principle — it forces students to state intent before coding.) 中 — Maieutic 作者花整整兩天做設計與技術規格、一行程式都沒寫——這個「慢」的開頭讓後面全部變快。（這產品本身就是這條原則：逼學生先講意圖再寫程式。）

4. Evals before features｜先做評估，後做功能

MaestrIA → W8 / W16 (Judge Skill) / W13 (validity)

EN — MaestrIA built a 9-dimension auditable eval against 12 real cases (ground truth recorded by the founder's father) — and let the eval, not intuition, steer development. This is the course's Judge Skill, arrived at independently. 中 — MaestrIA 建了一套9 維、對照 12 個真實案例的可審計評估（基準事實由作者父親記錄），讓評估而非直覺主導開發方向。這就是課程的 Judge Skill，自己摸索出來的版本。

5. Let Claude audit｜讓 Claude 審計

ARIA → W10 / W11 (PRD stress test)

EN — ARIA's rule: before building the next feature, ask Claude to find any problems in what's already built. Build the self-check into the loop, don't inspect by hand afterwards. (Echoes Tekton's independent verifier in Claude Build Day Hackathon.) 中 — ARIA 的做法：建下一個功能前，先請 Claude 找出已建內容的問題。把自我檢查設計進迴圈，而非事後人工抽查。（呼應 Claude Build Day Hackathon 中 Tekton 的獨立驗證者。）

6. Budget time for the demo｜替 demo 留時間

Virtual Puppet Theater → W8 / W16 (pitch)

EN — Recording a 3-minute demo video took far longer than expected. A product no one can see clearly doesn't win — communication is part of the build. 中 — 錄一支 3 分鐘 demo 影片比預期久得多。沒人看得懂的產品不會贏——溝通本身就是建造的一部分。

Domain knowledge is the moat 領域知識才是護城河

EN — Notice what the strongest projects share: the AI was generic, but the domain knowledge was not. MaestrIA distilled diagnostic rules, local materials, trade jargon, and baseline prices from interviewing a tradesman father; ARIA encoded a senior engineer's intuition; Medkit embedded clinical guidelines. The defensible part wasn't the code Claude wrote — it was the human-sourced domain knowledge fed into it. (Direct tie to W14 stakeholder/expert sourcing and the course's "calibrated trust" thesis.)

中 — 注意最強專案的共同點：AI 是通用的，但領域知識不是。MaestrIA 的診斷規則、在地材料、行業術語、基準價是訪談當技師的父親提煉出來的；ARIA 編碼了資深工程師的直覺；Medkit 嵌入臨床指引。有護城河的不是 Claude 寫的程式碼，而是餵進去、來自人的領域知識。（直接連到 W14 利害關係人/專家訪談，與課程「校準信任」的主軸。）

One-line takeaway 一句話總結

EN — The tools were the same for everyone (Opus 4.7, Claude Code). The winners differed in how they worked with the AI: think first, distrust and push back, eval before feature, let it audit, and bring domain knowledge no model has. The interaction method is the skill — and it is teachable. 中 — 工具大家都一樣（Opus 4.7、Claude Code），得獎者的差別在怎麼跟 AI 協作：先想、不輕信並推回、先評估再做功能、讓它審計、帶進模型沒有的領域知識。互動工法才是能力——而且可以教。
