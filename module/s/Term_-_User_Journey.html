User Journey｜使用者旅程（User Journey / Customer Journey）

The path a person takes from not knowing your product exists to deciding whether to come back, broken into stages you can observe separately.

把一個人從不知道你的產品、到用完之後會不會回來，拆成一段一段可以分別觀察的過程。

What this term means｜這個詞在說什麼

The value is not the diagram; it is that each stage asks a different question. Someone in awareness is asking "what is this?"; someone in consideration is asking "how is it different?"; someone using it is asking "how do I do this?". Content strategy is preparing an answer for each stage.

It is often confused with the product lifecycle: a journey is what the user goes through, a lifecycle is what the product goes through.

旅程圖的價值不在圖，在每一階問的問題不一樣：認知階段的人在問「這是什麼」，考慮階段在問「跟別的差在哪」，使用階段在問「怎麼做到」。內容策略就是替每一階準備對應的東西。

它和產品生命週期常被混用：旅程講的是使用者經歷什麼，生命週期講的是產品經歷什麼。

Common misconceptions｜常見誤解

  • Drawing the journey you wish users took rather than the one they take. It has to come from interviews or behavioural data.

  • Stopping the map at purchase. Retention and return are the stages that decide whether the product survives.

  • 把旅程畫成自己希望的樣子，而不是使用者實際走的樣子。旅程要來自訪談或行為資料。

  • 只畫到「購買」就停。留下與回訪那一段才決定產品能不能活。

Related terms｜相關詞

Persona、Product Lifecycle、MRD
