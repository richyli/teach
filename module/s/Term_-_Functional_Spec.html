Functional Spec｜功能規格書（Functional Specification (FSD)）

The engineering-side description of how a requirement will actually be implemented.

從工程角度描述「這條需求實際上要怎麼被實作」的文件。

What this term means｜這個詞在說什麼

The PRD says what has to be true; the functional spec says how it is going to be made true. The first belongs to the product side, the second usually to engineering.

In small teams the two are often merged into one document. That is fine — but know that you merged two kinds of responsibility, so that when something breaks you know who to talk to.

PRD 說「要做到什麼」，功能規格說「打算怎麼做到」。前者由產品端負責，後者通常由工程端負責。

在小團隊裡這兩份常常合成一份，這沒問題——但你要知道自己合併了兩種責任，出事時才知道該找誰談。

Common misconceptions｜常見誤解

  • The product manager writing the functional spec and handing it to engineers to execute. That is not division of labour; it is taking their authority and their accountability at the same time.

  • 產品經理自己寫功能規格，然後工程師照做。那不是分工，是越權加卸責。
