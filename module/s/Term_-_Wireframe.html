Wireframe｜線框圖

Wireframe

A deliberately ugly layout sketch that shows structure and behaviour, not colour and type.

刻意做得很醜的版面草圖，只表達結構與行為，不表達顏色與字體。

What this term means｜這個詞在說什麼

The ugliness is the feature. Once you add colour and good typography the discussion slides towards "do you like it?", when what this stage needs is "is this in the right place, and what happens when I press it?".

Fidelity is a ladder: sketch → wireframe → high-fidelity mockup → clickable prototype. Climb it too fast and you will get feedback about colour instead of feedback about structure.

醜是功能不是缺點：一旦加上顏色與精美字體，討論就會滑向「好不好看」，而這個階段要討論的是「東西擺在這裡對不對、按下去會發生什麼」。

保真度是一把梯子：手繪 → 線框 → 高保真 mockup → 可點原型。爬得太快，你會拿到關於顏色的回饋，而不是關於結構的回饋。

Common misconceptions｜常見誤解

  • Asking "do you like it?" about a wireframe. Ask "where would you click first, and what do you expect to happen?".

  • Using wireframes as a substitute for the PRD. The picture shows the shape; the PRD states the rules.

  • 用線框圖去問「你喜歡嗎」。要問的是「你會先點哪裡、你預期會發生什麼」。

  • 把線框圖當成 PRD 的替代品。圖說明長相，PRD 說明規則。
