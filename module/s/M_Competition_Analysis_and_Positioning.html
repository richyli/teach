Competition Analysis and Positioning

This unit covers SWOT and TOWS, the positioning map, and how generative AI is dissolving the moats that content companies used to rely on.

Core

  • SWOT and TOWS; Crossing the Chasm; the positioning map.
  • How generative AI changes competitive moats: data, distribution, and workflow lock-in.
  • Primary theory: Dynamic Capabilities (Teece). Supporting: Diffusion of Innovations — where a rival sits on the adoption curve decides what competition with them looks like.

Role play — Competitor Persona Switch

Your team is assigned an existing content company: the New York Times, the BBC, Bloomberg, Mirror Media, CNA, or similar.

  1. Become that company and respond to a hypothetical AI-native disruption announcement
  2. Produce a CEO statement plus three strategic moves
  3. The class judges which company defended its position most convincingly

✍️ Take-home task

Submit your positioning map plus a summary of that company's defensive strategy.

💬 Discussion and reflection

Pair review

Swap positioning maps and defensive strategies with another team.

  1. Find the fatal gap: what single move by a competitor would make their company indefensible?
  2. Use their own SWOT or TOWS to show the threat they overlooked, or the strength they overrated
  3. Say which of the three moats — data, distribution, workflow lock-in — their strategy actually depends on

Individual reflection

Write one sentence to keep: "If I were the CEO of this company, the first thing I would do tomorrow is ______" — then mark whether it is sensing, seizing, or transforming.
