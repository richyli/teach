User Research and MRD Assembly

This unit covers journey and jobs maps, AI-assisted research synthesis, and how four weeks of separate findings get assembled into one document you have to defend.

Core

  • User-journey map and jobs map; interview versus survey.
  • AI-assisted synthesis of user research (Dovetail-style clustering).
  • Primary theory: Boundary Object Theory (Star & Griesemer) — the MRD is the team's first cross-functional boundary object. Supporting: Jobs-to-be-Done (who it is for), Sensemaking (the collective interpretation needed to write it).

Assembling the MRD

Market sizing from W4, the competitive matrix from W5, the target segment from W6, and this week's persona and journey become a single document. Add the Business Model Canvas and the positioning statement. The MRD is finished as homework and defended in W8.

✍️ Take-home task

Complete the first full version of the MRD, for the W8 midterm pitch.

💬 Discussion and reflection

Pair review

Swap jobs maps or user-journey maps with a partner.

  1. Find one job that you think is not what the user is actually trying to get done
  2. Ask them what the evidence for that journey is — an interview, or an assumption
  3. Mark the section of their map that has the least evidence behind it

Individual reflection

Write one sentence to keep: "The weakest, most imagined part of my MRD is __, and before the deadline I will back it with ____" — an interview, a competitor check, or market data.
